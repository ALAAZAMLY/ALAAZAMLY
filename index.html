The Person Behind the Pixels

Always asking why. Then asking again.

For more than three years I’ve designed websites and mobile apps for brands in very different corners of the market. Somewhere along the way I stopped thinking about screens and started thinking about people: the tired parent ordering dinner, the shopper who quietly gives up at checkout. My job is to make their next step obvious, and a little nicer than they expected.
